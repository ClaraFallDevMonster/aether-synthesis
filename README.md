# Aether Synthesis – Hand-Controlled Particle Field

Aether Synthesis is an interactive web-based particle sculpture that responds to **your hands** as if they were instruments.  
With **MediaPipe Hands** and **Three.js**, your gestures become forces that expand, distort, and recolor a living field of light.

---

## About / Why

Aether Synthesis was created as an exploration of **gesture as language** and **light as matter**.  
The particles behave like a subtle medium—something between nebula and thought—shaped not by clicks or keys, but by presence.

It is a small experiment in *human–digital synesthesia*:  
a place where movement becomes geometry, intention becomes color,  
and the boundary between controller and creation dissolves for a moment.

---

## Gestures & Interaction

HANDS APART — Expand
Increase the distance between your hands → the particle field stretches outward.

PINCH — Color Shift
Thumb + index pinch → triggers a new random hue.

WAVE — Repel
Move your hands through the field → particles scatter and push away.

---

## Technology

Three.js for rendering a 3D particle cloud with custom GLSL shaders

Fragment & Vertex shaders for noise-driven motion, glow, and repulsion

MediaPipe Hands for real-time hand detection, world projection, and gesture logic

Pure HTML/CSS UI with holographic styling, glass surfaces, and scanline overlays


This entire prototype runs in a single HTML file.
