Aether Synthesis

A short guide to help you connect your hands to the particle field and get the most out of the experience.

1. Setup

Prepare your camera and position so the system can read your gestures clearly.

  • •Allow camera: Accept the webcam prompt to initialize hand tracking.
  • •Position: Sit back slightly so the camera can clearly see both of your hands.

2. Hand Gestures

Use simple gestures to sculpt the particles as if they were a responsive force field.

  • •👋 Wave / Hover: Move your hand over the particles to push and repel them.
  • •🙌 Two Hands (Accordion): Hold both hands up. Move them apart to expand the object and closer together to compress it.
  • •👌 Pinch: Pinch your thumb and index finger together to trigger a random color shift.

3. On-Screen Controls

Use the dock at the bottom of the screen to change the overall structure of the field.

  • •Shape menu: Click the buttons at the bottom (Saturn, Nova, Morpho, Lotus) to switch between particle formations.

Troubleshooting

If the particles don’t react to your hands, check these conditions:

  • •Your room is reasonably well-lit (avoid extremes of very dark or very bright).
  • •Your hands are visible in the holographic monitor in the top-right corner.
  • •The browser has permission to use your camera and the tab is active.
